1. Platform Overview

The AI-Based Visual Risk and Compliance Intelligence System is committed to preserving the privacy and data integrity of all users who interact with our personal decision-support algorithms. This Privacy Policy clarifies how personal identifiers, financial telemetry, academic study statistics, and wellness metrics are collected, synthesized, and secured within your digital twin environment.

Core Privacy Commitment

Your behavioral telemetry and lifestyle metrics are strictly utilized to compute personalized risk scores and simulation projections. We never monetize, lease, or distribute your raw decision logs to unauthorized external brokers.

2. Categories of Data Collected

To construct an accurate digital model and produce predictive foresight, our system processes the following data tiers:

Profile Identifiers

Username, email address, hashed credentials, occupation, age, baseline savings, and declared financial parameters.

Financial Transactions

Income allocations, expense categories, transaction timestamps, and net cashflow telemetry used for 6-month forecasting.

Study Intelligence

Study duration, academic subject classifications, self-reported productivity ratings, and consecutive learning streaks.

Fitness & Wellness Logs

Physical workout activities, active duration (minutes), estimated calories burned, and 150-minute weekly progress.

3. Machine Learning & AI Processing

Our platform applies machine learning (scikit-learn Linear Regression) and pattern analysis to generate projections. When interacting with the AI Assistant, conversational queries alongside aggregated user lifestyle metrics are evaluated using Google Gemini generative AI (or internal rule-based inference models when offline).

No confidential banking passwords or sensitive credentials are ever transmitted into the generative AI prompt context.

4. Security & Encryption Standards

We implement defense-in-depth measures to safeguard user records:

  • Cryptographic Password Hashing: Passwords are encrypted utilizing Werkzeug's secure hashing functions (`generate_password_hash` with PBKDF2/scrypt).
  • Session Management: Authenticated sessions are managed via Flask-Login with encrypted session cookies.
  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Administrative endpoints are strictly partitioned from user accounts through verified permissions.
  • Isolated Database Storage: SQLite database records are stored with constrained server permissions.

5. User Control & Data Rights

You maintain continuous ownership and governance over your digital representation:

Right Mechanism
Inspection Review all transactions, study sessions, and fitness activities on the Personal Dashboard.
Rectification Edit any logged transaction, study record, or fitness entry through interactive modal dialogs.
Erasure Delete individual telemetry entries or clear entire simulation histories with one click.
